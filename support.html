Need assistance or have feedback about Scheduled Website Opener?

How do I schedule a website?
Click on the extension icon in your Chrome toolbar, press "+ Add New Schedule", enter your website URL, pick recurrence frequency (Daily, Weekly, Monthly, Once), set time, and click Save.
Will websites open if my laptop is in sleep mode?
If your computer is in sleep mode, Chrome will fire any pending alarms as soon as the system wakes up and Chrome resumes.
