Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions? We've got answers.

Does Chrome need to be open for scheduled websites to open? ↓
Yes, Google Chrome must be running in the background for alarms to trigger tab opening. If Chrome is closed at the scheduled time, the extension will open missed websites upon browser launch.
Is my browsing or schedule data uploaded to external servers? ↓
No, 100% of your data remains strictly local in your Google Chrome profile storage (`chrome.storage.local`). We collect zero personal data, telemetry, or URLs.
Is Scheduled Website Opener free to use? ↓
Yes, the extension is 100% free with full functionality and zero ads.
