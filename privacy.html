🔒 Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

🛡️ Privacy First Commitment

Scheduled Website Opener does not collect, track, or share any personal information. All your URL schedule rules stay 100% locally inside your browser profile.

1. Overview

This privacy policy explains how the Scheduled Website Opener Chrome extension handles user data. We are committed to complete privacy and transparency.

2. What Data We Collect

We collect zero personal information. The extension operates entirely offline and locally on your machine using Chrome's native extension APIs.

Local Data Storage

The following data is stored locally in your browser (`chrome.storage.local`):

  • Website URLs: The URLs you schedule to open.
  • Schedule Rules: The times and frequencies you specify.
  • Extension Preferences: Your active toggles and settings.

3. How Your Data is Processed

All schedule execution happens strictly on your computer. We do not operate external tracking servers or databases. Data is processed using:

  • chrome.storage.local — Stores schedule entries locally on your device.
  • chrome.alarms — Manages browser timers for URL opening triggers.
  • chrome.tabs — Opens scheduled URLs in browser tabs when alarms fire.

4. Permissions Used & Purpose

Storage Permission

Purpose: To save your scheduled website list and preferences locally.
Sharing: Never shared or transmitted anywhere.

Alarms Permission

Purpose: To fire timing events that launch your requested tabs.
Sharing: Processed locally inside Chrome.

Tabs Permission

Purpose: To open your scheduled website links in new browser tabs.
Sharing: Never shared.
